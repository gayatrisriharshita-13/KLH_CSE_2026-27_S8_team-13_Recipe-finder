Potato Fry
Ingredients: potato, spices, oil, mustard seeds, salt
Amounts: 3 potatoes, 1 tsp spices, 2 tbsp oil, 1 tsp mustard seeds, half tsp salt
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 15
TotalTime: 25
Servings: 3
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Lunch
Diet: Vegan
Instructions: Cut the potatoes into small cubes. Splutter mustard seeds in hot oil. Add potatoes, spices and salt. Cover and cook on low heat, stirring now and then, until crisp.
