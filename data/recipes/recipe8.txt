Aloo Paratha
Ingredients: wheat flour, potato, spices, butter, salt
Amounts: 2 cups wheat flour, 3 potatoes, 1 tsp spices, 1 tbsp butter, half tsp salt
PreparationTime: 20
CookingTime: 15
TotalTime: 35
Servings: 3
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Breakfast
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Boil and mash the potatoes with spices and salt. Knead a soft dough with the flour. Stuff the dough with the filling and roll flat. Cook on a hot pan with butter until golden on both sides.
