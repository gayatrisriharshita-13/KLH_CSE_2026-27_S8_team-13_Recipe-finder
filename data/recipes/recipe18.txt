Vegetable Soup
Ingredients: carrot, beans, onion, tomato, garlic, pepper, butter
Amounts: 1 carrot, half cup beans, 1 onion, 1 tomato, 2 cloves garlic, half tsp pepper, 1 tbsp butter
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 20
TotalTime: 30
Servings: 3
Cuisine: Continental
Category: Lunch
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Saute garlic and onion in butter. Add chopped carrot, beans and tomato. Pour in three cups of water and simmer for 15 minutes. Season with pepper and salt.
