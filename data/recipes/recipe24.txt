Curd Rice
Ingredients: rice, curd, mustard seeds, green chilli, oil
Amounts: 1 cup cooked rice, 1 cup curd, 1 tsp mustard seeds, 1 green chilli, 1 tbsp oil
PreparationTime: 5
CookingTime: 5
TotalTime: 10
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Lunch
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Mash the rice lightly and mix with curd and salt. Splutter mustard seeds and chopped chilli in hot oil. Pour over the rice and mix well. Serve cool.
