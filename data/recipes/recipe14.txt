Chicken Curry
Ingredients: chicken, tomato, onion, garlic, ginger, spices, oil
Amounts: 500 g chicken, 2 tomatoes, 2 onions, 3 cloves garlic, 1 inch ginger, 2 tsp spices, 3 tbsp oil
PreparationTime: 15
CookingTime: 35
TotalTime: 50
Servings: 4
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Dinner
Diet: Non-Vegetarian
Instructions: Brown the onions in oil. Add garlic, ginger and spices. Add tomatoes and cook to a paste. Add the chicken with a little water. Cover and simmer until the chicken is cooked through.
