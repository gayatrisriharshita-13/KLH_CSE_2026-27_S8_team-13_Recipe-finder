Masala Chai
Ingredients: water, milk, tea, ginger, sugar
Amounts: 2 cups water, 1 cup milk, 2 tsp tea leaves, 1 inch ginger, 2 tsp sugar
PreparationTime: 2
CookingTime: 8
TotalTime: 10
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Snack
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Boil water with crushed ginger and tea leaves. Add milk and sugar. Simmer for 3 minutes, strain into cups and serve hot.
