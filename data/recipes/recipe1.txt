Tomato Pasta
Ingredients: tomato, pasta, garlic, onion, olive oil, salt
Amounts: 2 tomatoes, 200 g pasta, 4 cloves garlic, 1 onion, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp salt
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 15
TotalTime: 25
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Italian
Category: Lunch
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Boil the pasta until tender. Heat olive oil in a pan and saute garlic and onion. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft. Add the cooked pasta and mix well. Add salt and seasoning. Cook for another 2 to 3 minutes and serve hot.
