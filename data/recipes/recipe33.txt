Bread Upma
Ingredients: bread, onion, tomato, mustard seeds, oil
Amounts: 4 bread slices, 1 onion, 1 tomato, 1 tsp mustard seeds, 1 tbsp oil
PreparationTime: 5
CookingTime: 8
TotalTime: 13
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Breakfast
Diet: Vegan
Instructions: Cut the bread into small cubes. Splutter mustard seeds in oil and saute onion and tomato. Add the bread, salt and a sprinkle of water. Toss until soft and well coated.
