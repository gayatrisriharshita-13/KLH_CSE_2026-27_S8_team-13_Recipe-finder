Cheese Omelette
Ingredients: egg, cheese, butter, pepper
Amounts: 2 eggs, 2 cheese slices, 1 tbsp butter, a pinch of pepper
PreparationTime: 3
CookingTime: 4
TotalTime: 7
Servings: 1
Cuisine: Continental
Category: Breakfast
Diet: Non-Vegetarian
Instructions: Whisk the eggs with pepper. Melt butter in a pan and pour in the eggs. Place cheese on one half when nearly set. Fold over and cook for another minute.
