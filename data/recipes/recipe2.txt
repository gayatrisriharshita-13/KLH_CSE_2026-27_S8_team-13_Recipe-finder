Tomato Sandwich
Ingredients: tomato, onion, cheese, bread, butter
Amounts: 1 tomato, 1 onion, 2 cheese slices, 4 bread slices, 1 tbsp butter
PreparationTime: 5
CookingTime: 5
TotalTime: 10
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Continental
Category: Snack
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Slice the tomato and onion. Spread butter on the bread slices. Add tomato, onion and cheese between the bread slices. Heat a pan and toast the sandwich until the bread becomes golden and the cheese melts. Serve hot.
