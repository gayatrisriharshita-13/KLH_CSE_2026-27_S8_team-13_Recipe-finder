Lemon Rice
Ingredients: rice, lemon, peanuts, turmeric, mustard seeds, oil
Amounts: 2 cups cooked rice, 1 lemon, 1 handful peanuts, half tsp turmeric, 1 tsp mustard seeds, 1 tbsp oil
PreparationTime: 5
CookingTime: 10
TotalTime: 15
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Lunch
Diet: Vegan
Instructions: Fry mustard seeds and peanuts in oil. Add turmeric and the rice. Mix well and cook for 2 minutes. Turn off the heat and stir in lemon juice and salt.
