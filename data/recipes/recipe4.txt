Cheese Toast
Ingredients: cheese, bread, butter, oregano, salt
Amounts: 4 bread slices, 4 cheese slices, 1 tbsp butter, 1 tsp oregano, half tsp salt
PreparationTime: 5
CookingTime: 10
TotalTime: 15
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Continental
Category: Breakfast
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Spread butter on the bread slices. Place cheese on each slice. Sprinkle oregano and a little salt. Toast the bread in a pan or oven until the cheese melts and the bread becomes crispy. Serve immediately.
