Veg Noodles
Ingredients: noodles, carrot, cabbage, onion, soy sauce, oil
Amounts: 200 g noodles, 1 carrot, half cup cabbage, 1 onion, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp oil
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 10
TotalTime: 20
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Chinese
Category: Dinner
Diet: Vegan
Instructions: Boil the noodles and drain. Stir fry sliced onion, carrot and cabbage in oil on high heat. Add the noodles and soy sauce. Toss well for 2 minutes and serve hot.
