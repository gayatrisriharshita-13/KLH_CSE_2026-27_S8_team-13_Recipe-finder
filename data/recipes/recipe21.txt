Banana Milkshake
Ingredients: banana, milk, sugar
Amounts: 2 bananas, 2 cups milk, 2 tsp sugar
PreparationTime: 5
CookingTime: 0
TotalTime: 5
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Continental
Category: Snack
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Chop the bananas. Blend with cold milk and sugar until smooth. Pour into glasses and serve chilled.
