Rice Kheer
Ingredients: rice, milk, sugar, cardamom, nuts
Amounts: half cup rice, 1 litre milk, 3 tbsp sugar, 4 cardamom pods, 1 handful nuts
PreparationTime: 5
CookingTime: 35
TotalTime: 40
Servings: 4
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Dessert
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Wash the rice and boil it in the milk on low heat. Stir often until thick and creamy. Add sugar and crushed cardamom. Cook 5 more minutes and top with nuts.
