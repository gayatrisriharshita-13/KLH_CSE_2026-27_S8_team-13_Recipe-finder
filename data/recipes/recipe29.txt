Fruit Chaat
Ingredients: apple, banana, grapes, spices, lemon
Amounts: 1 apple, 1 banana, 1 cup grapes, 1 tsp chaat masala, 1 lemon
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 0
TotalTime: 10
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Snack
Diet: Vegan
Instructions: Chop the apple and banana. Add the grapes. Sprinkle chaat masala and a squeeze of lemon. Toss and serve right away.
