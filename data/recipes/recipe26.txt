Fish Fry
Ingredients: fish, spices, lemon, oil, salt
Amounts: 4 fish fillets, 1 tsp spices, 1 lemon, 3 tbsp oil, half tsp salt
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 10
TotalTime: 20
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Dinner
Diet: Non-Vegetarian
Instructions: Marinate the fish with spices, lemon juice and salt for 10 minutes. Heat oil in a pan. Shallow fry the fish for 4 to 5 minutes on each side until crisp.
