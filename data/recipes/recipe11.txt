Jeera Rice
Ingredients: rice, cumin, ghee, salt
Amounts: 1 cup rice, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tbsp ghee, half tsp salt
PreparationTime: 5
CookingTime: 15
TotalTime: 20
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Lunch
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Wash the rice and soak for 15 minutes. Heat ghee and fry the cumin. Add the rice, salt and two cups of water. Cook covered until the water is absorbed.
