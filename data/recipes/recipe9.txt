Dal Tadka
Ingredients: lentils, tomato, onion, garlic, cumin, ghee, turmeric
Amounts: 1 cup lentils, 1 tomato, 1 onion, 3 cloves garlic, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tbsp ghee, half tsp turmeric
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 25
TotalTime: 35
Servings: 3
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Dinner
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Boil the lentils with turmeric until soft. Heat ghee and splutter cumin. Saute garlic, onion and tomato. Pour this tadka over the dal, simmer for 5 minutes and serve with rice.
