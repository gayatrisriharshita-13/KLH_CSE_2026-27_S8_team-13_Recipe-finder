Masala Omelette
Ingredients: egg, onion, tomato, green chilli, oil, salt
Amounts: 2 eggs, 1 onion, 1 tomato, 1 green chilli, 1 tbsp oil, half tsp salt
PreparationTime: 5
CookingTime: 5
TotalTime: 10
Servings: 1
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Breakfast
Diet: Non-Vegetarian
Instructions: Beat the eggs with chopped onion, tomato, chilli and salt. Heat oil in a pan. Pour in the mixture and cook until set. Flip once and serve hot.
