Chana Masala
Ingredients: chickpeas, tomato, onion, garlic, spices, oil
Amounts: 1 cup chickpeas, 2 tomatoes, 1 onion, 2 cloves garlic, 1 tsp spices, 2 tbsp oil
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 25
TotalTime: 35
Servings: 3
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Dinner
Diet: Vegan
Instructions: Boil the soaked chickpeas until tender. Saute onion and garlic in oil. Add tomatoes and spices and cook to a thick paste. Add the chickpeas with a little water and simmer for 10 minutes.
