Banana Pancakes
Ingredients: banana, wheat flour, egg, milk, butter
Amounts: 2 bananas, 1 cup wheat flour, 1 egg, 1 cup milk, 1 tbsp butter
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 10
TotalTime: 20
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Continental
Category: Breakfast
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Mash the bananas and mix with egg and milk. Stir in the flour to make a batter. Cook small ladles on a buttered pan until bubbles form. Flip and cook until golden.
