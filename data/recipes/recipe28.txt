Greek Salad
Ingredients: cucumber, tomato, onion, cheese, olive oil, oregano
Amounts: 2 cucumbers, 2 tomatoes, 1 onion, 100 g feta, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp oregano
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 0
TotalTime: 10
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Mediterranean
Category: Lunch
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Chop the cucumbers, tomatoes and onion into chunks. Add cubes of feta. Drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle oregano and toss gently.
