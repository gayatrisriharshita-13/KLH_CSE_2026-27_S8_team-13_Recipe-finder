Spaghetti Aglio e Olio
Ingredients: pasta, garlic, olive oil, chilli flakes, salt
Amounts: 200 g pasta, 5 cloves garlic, 3 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp chilli flakes, half tsp salt
PreparationTime: 5
CookingTime: 12
TotalTime: 17
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Italian
Category: Dinner
Diet: Vegan
Instructions: Boil the pasta in salted water. Warm olive oil and gently cook sliced garlic and chilli flakes. Toss in the drained pasta with a spoon of pasta water. Serve at once.
