Poha
Ingredients: poha, onion, potato, peanuts, turmeric, oil, lemon
Amounts: 1 cup flattened rice, 1 onion, 1 potato, 1 handful peanuts, half tsp turmeric, 1 tbsp oil, 1 lemon
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 10
TotalTime: 20
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Breakfast
Diet: Vegan
Instructions: Rinse the poha and drain well. Fry peanuts, onion and diced potato in oil. Add turmeric and salt, then the poha. Mix gently, steam for 2 minutes and finish with lemon juice.
