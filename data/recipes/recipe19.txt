Tomato Soup
Ingredients: tomato, onion, garlic, butter, pepper
Amounts: 4 tomatoes, 1 onion, 2 cloves garlic, 1 tbsp butter, half tsp pepper
PreparationTime: 5
CookingTime: 20
TotalTime: 25
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Continental
Category: Snack
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Saute onion and garlic in butter. Add chopped tomatoes and two cups of water. Simmer for 15 minutes, then blend smooth. Season with pepper and salt and serve hot.
