Garlic Bread
Ingredients: bread, butter, garlic, oregano
Amounts: 4 bread slices, 3 tbsp butter, 3 cloves garlic, 1 tsp oregano
PreparationTime: 5
CookingTime: 8
TotalTime: 13
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Italian
Category: Snack
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Mash the butter with crushed garlic and oregano. Spread it on the bread slices. Toast in a hot pan or oven until crisp and golden.
