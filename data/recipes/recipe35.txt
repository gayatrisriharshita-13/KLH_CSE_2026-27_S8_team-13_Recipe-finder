Paneer Bhurji
Ingredients: paneer, onion, tomato, green chilli, spices, oil
Amounts: 200 g paneer, 1 onion, 1 tomato, 1 green chilli, 1 tsp spices, 1 tbsp oil
PreparationTime: 5
CookingTime: 10
TotalTime: 15
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Dinner
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Crumble the paneer. Saute onion, chilli and tomato in oil. Add spices and the paneer. Cook for 3 minutes and serve with bread.
