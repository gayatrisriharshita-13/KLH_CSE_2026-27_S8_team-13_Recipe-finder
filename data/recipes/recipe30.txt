Egg Curry
Ingredients: egg, tomato, onion, spices, oil, garlic
Amounts: 4 boiled eggs, 2 tomatoes, 2 onions, 2 tsp spices, 2 tbsp oil, 2 cloves garlic
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 20
TotalTime: 30
Servings: 3
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Dinner
Diet: Non-Vegetarian
Instructions: Saute onion and garlic in oil. Add tomatoes and spices and cook to a thick gravy. Add a cup of water and the halved eggs. Simmer for 5 minutes and serve.
