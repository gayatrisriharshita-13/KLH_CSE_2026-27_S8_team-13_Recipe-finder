Margherita Pizza
Ingredients: pizza base, tomato, cheese, basil, olive oil
Amounts: 1 pizza base, 3 tbsp tomato sauce, 1 cup mozzarella, a few basil leaves, 1 tbsp olive oil
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 12
TotalTime: 22
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Italian
Category: Dinner
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Spread tomato sauce over the pizza base. Top with cheese and drizzle with olive oil. Bake at 220 C for 10 to 12 minutes until the cheese bubbles. Scatter basil on top and serve.
