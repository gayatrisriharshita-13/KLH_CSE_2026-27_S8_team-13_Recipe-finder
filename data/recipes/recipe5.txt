Vegetable Fried Rice
Ingredients: rice, carrot, beans, onion, garlic, soy sauce, oil
Amounts: 2 cups cooked rice, 1 carrot, half cup beans, 1 onion, 2 cloves garlic, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp oil
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 20
TotalTime: 30
Servings: 3
Cuisine: Chinese
Category: Lunch
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Heat oil in a wok. Add chopped garlic and onion and saute for a minute. Add carrot and beans and cook until slightly tender. Add cooked rice and mix well. Add soy sauce and salt. Stir fry everything on high heat for a few minutes and serve hot.
