Vegetable Pulao
Ingredients: rice, carrot, beans, onion, spices, ghee
Amounts: 1 cup rice, 1 carrot, half cup beans, 1 onion, 1 tsp spices, 2 tbsp ghee
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 20
TotalTime: 30
Servings: 3
Cuisine: Indian
Category: Lunch
Diet: Vegetarian
Instructions: Fry sliced onion in ghee. Add carrot, beans and spices. Add the washed rice and two cups of water. Cover and cook until the rice is fluffy.
