Hummus
Ingredients: chickpeas, tahini, garlic, lemon, olive oil
Amounts: 1 cup chickpeas, 2 tbsp tahini, 2 cloves garlic, 1 lemon, 2 tbsp olive oil
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 0
TotalTime: 10
Servings: 4
Cuisine: Middle Eastern
Category: Snack
Diet: Vegan
Instructions: Blend boiled chickpeas with tahini, garlic, lemon juice and salt. Add a splash of water until creamy. Drizzle with olive oil and serve with bread.
