Egg Fried Rice
Ingredients: rice, egg, carrot, garlic, soy sauce, oil
Amounts: 2 cups cooked rice, 2 eggs, 1 carrot, 2 cloves garlic, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp oil
PreparationTime: 10
CookingTime: 10
TotalTime: 20
Servings: 2
Cuisine: Chinese
Category: Lunch
Diet: Non-Vegetarian
Instructions: Scramble the eggs in hot oil and set aside. Fry garlic and grated carrot for a minute. Add the rice and soy sauce and toss on high heat. Fold the eggs back in and serve.
